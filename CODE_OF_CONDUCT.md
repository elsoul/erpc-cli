# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, and focus
feedback on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior harms the community. Reports should use a private repository
contact channel when confidentiality is needed.
